What Pharma AI Does

Everyday questions and drafting, department by department.

Pharma AI gives each department a focused AI assistant for everyday questions and drafting. Users get drafts to review. They never get decisions.

  • 19 standard departments covering the complete structure of a pharmaceutical company. Corporate / Office: Administration / Management; HR; Finance & Accounts; Sales & Marketing, Business Development; Purchase / Procurement / Commercial; Supply Chain; Planning / PPC; and Legal & Regulatory Affairs. Manufacturing Plant: Production & Operations; QA / QC / Validation; Research & Development; Warehouse / Stores; Engineering, Maintenance & Projects; EHS / Safety; and Facility Management. Common: Security; IT; Housekeeping; and Canteen. The Administrator switches each department on or off.
  • Domain Guard checks every question with fast rules, a small classifier model and an output check. A question from outside the department gets a polite refusal that names the right department, and the main model is never called for it. No department answers requests for software code, and emergency questions get fixed emergency guidance first.
  • Quick Create cards for SOP, Logsheet, Checklist, Manual, Datasheet, Write a mail, Presentation, Excel sheet, Chart and Drawing. Each card opens a short guided form, and the chat then produces the file.
  • Word, PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, chart, drawing and e-mail (.eml) files. Word, PDF and PowerPoint files carry your company logo and name, the department, the user, the date and an “AI-assisted draft – review before use” note. Excel sheets, charts and drawings carry the same details as text, and e-mail drafts carry the note in the file's header fields.
  • A sandboxed preview panel with no scripts, macros or network. Download is the only action, and every download is logged.
  • Write a mail drafts workplace e-mails on any topic. You copy the draft or download it as .eml or Word, then send it from your own mail program. The app has no send control.
  • Upload for review accepts PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, CSV, PNG and JPG files (50 MB by default, up to 1 GB where your server allows), with a reason, a description and the Level-2 password. Macro files and programs are refused. The original is stored on your server with its SHA-256 fingerprint, and the Administrator is notified.
  • Chat answers stay only in the PC's memory and are gone at sign-out. By default your server keeps only each answer's SHA-256 fingerprint, length, tokens and model, not its text.
21 CFR Part 11 · EU GMP Annex 11

Functions built on 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 requirements.

Pharma AI's functions are built on the electronic-record requirements of 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11, and support ALCOA+ data-integrity principles and GAMP 5 guidance. In our clause-by-clause assessment, the controls for system access, authority checks, device checks, audit trails and record copies are assessed as supported, and step sequencing as supported where applicable. Protecting records over the whole retention period, archiving and data migration are partly supported, as explained under “About certification” below. Pharma AI has no electronic signatures, so Part 11 Subpart C does not apply, and the AI never approves, signs or releases records. Built-in controls and supplier evidence:

  • A secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trail. It records every sign-in, question (with its decision and reason), result, upload, download, admin action, deletion and legal hold, with the user, the PC and the server time (stored in UTC, shown in IST). The database refuses any change to a log record and allows deletion only through the controlled Administrator deletion or the nightly retention job, both recorded in the Deletion Log.
  • Named personal logins created only by the Administrator, with one department per user, no self-registration and no self-service password reset.
  • Role-based access (User, Admin, Backup Admin) enforced on the server. Administrators can never read passwords, Level-2 passwords or AI keys; they can only reset them.
  • Account lockout after wrong passwords (default 5), with Administrator notification, idle sign-out (default 15 minutes) and a maximum session length (default 12 hours).
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app for Administrators. Once an Administrator has enrolled, every Administrator function requires it.
  • Password re-authentication for critical actions: a deletion needs a written reason and the Administrator's own password, an upload needs the Level-2 password, and showing the PC connection code needs the Administrator's Level-2 password and is logged. These are password checks; Pharma AI has no electronic signatures.
  • Controlled deletion by Administrators only. Every deletion goes to a permanent Deletion Log and, by default, notifies all Administrators. Admin actions and the Deletion Log can never be deleted.
  • Retention set by the Administrator (default 1,095 days / 3 years; 30 to 36,500 days) and applied by a nightly job at 02:00 IST. Legal holds stop deletion.
  • Copies for review: filtered audit exports to Excel, PDF or CSV, and a full JSON export of all 22 record types. Each export is itself logged with its SHA-256. Uploaded files stay on your server and are listed in the export, not included, and AI answer text is stored only if your Administrator switches answer storage on.
  • PC-bound sessions: each PC joins with the PC connection code and registers a DPAPI-protected device key. A lost PC can be released at once.
  • ALCOA+ support: records are attributable (user, role, department, PC, server time, AI provider and model), contemporaneous, original (uploads with SHA-256) and complete (refused questions are kept too).
  • Change control support: Windows website installs update only after the Administrator approves the version, Store installs are updated by the Store, Macs are updated only when the new .dmg is installed, and every configuration change is recorded.
  • Supplier evidence for your validation: a URS with 121 requirements, a traceability matrix, a verification report and a clause-by-clause Part 11 / Annex 11 assessment. The suggested GAMP 5 category is Category 4 (configured product) with indirect GxP impact; your company confirms this in its validation plan.
About Certification

Asked for a Part 11 certificate? Here is the answer.

Does Pharma AI have a 21 CFR Part 11 certificate? +

No. The FDA and the EMA do not certify or approve software for 21 CFR Part 11 or EU GMP Annex 11 compliance, so no software can hold an official “Part 11 certificate” or “Annex 11 certificate”. A pharmaceutical company achieves compliance by validating the system for its intended use and running it under its own SOPs and controls. N Automations supplies the complete supplier validation pack to make that fast: a User Requirement Specification with 121 requirements, a requirements traceability matrix, a software verification and validation report, and a clause-by-clause 21 CFR Part 11 / EU GMP Annex 11 Regulatory Compliance Assessment. Write to info@nautomations.in to receive it.

How far does Pharma AI meet Part 11 and Annex 11? +

Its functions are built on both. In our clause-by-clause Regulatory Compliance Assessment, the Part 11 controls for limited system access (11.10 d), audit trails (11.10 e), authority checks (11.10 g), device checks (11.10 h) and record copies (11.10 b, with one stated limitation: AI answer text is kept only if your Administrator switches answer storage on) are assessed as supported, and step sequencing (11.10 f) as supported where applicable. For Annex 11, data-exchange checks (5), printed copies through exports (8.1), the audit trail (9), logical access and security controls (12.1 to 12.4) and business continuity (16) are assessed as supported. Three areas are partly supported: protecting records for the whole retention period (11.10 c; Annex 11 clause 7.1) and archiving (Annex 11 clause 17), which also depend on your company's hosting, backups and archives; and data migration (Annex 11 clause 4.8), because a restore tool for the full data export is not part of version 1.1.0. Pharma AI has no electronic signatures, so Part 11 Subpart C does not apply. Validation, training, written policies, document control, backups and the classification of your deployment remain your company's responsibility under its own quality system; the assessment gives practical guidance for each. The assessment covers Pharma AI 1.1.0 for Windows. The macOS version has the same functions and uses the same company server, but it was not assessed separately.

How It Works

Your server, your AI key.

Pharma AI keeps your company's data under your company's control. In daily use, PCs talk only to your company server, and your company server talks to the AI provider you choose.

Pharma AI on Windows and macOS. PCs and Macs connect only to your company server, over HTTPS and WSS.

02

Your company server

Supabase, set up automatically, or self-hosted. Accounts, records, files, settings and AI keys are stored only here.

03

Your AI provider

OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini, called only from your server with your own key.

Licence data only

Security & Privacy

Security and privacy.

  • No share, send, e-mail or print controls; no connectors, plug-ins, web browsing or code execution; and no telemetry, analytics or crash reports.
  • PCs connect only to your company server, over HTTPS and WSS. During setup or a company-server update, the Administrator's PC also reaches the N Automations Licence Service and api.supabase.com. Windows PCs installed from the website also fetch the signed update file from www.nautomations.in when an update is checked or approved; Macs fetch no update file.
  • Integrity: SHA-256 fingerprints of answers, uploads and downloaded files; licence data signed with Ed25519; requests checked against strict schemas; and TLS for all traffic.
  • Hardened 1.1.0 build: a compiled core without debug information, encrypted sensitive texts, obfuscated setup, sign-in and licence screens, an integrity check at every start (on a Mac, of the bundled server files and guides) and debugger detection. No software can be guaranteed impossible to attack; our Application Security Hardening Statement explains the protections and their limits.
  • N Automations receives only licence data: the Administrator's company e-mail address and its domain, your company server address, the app version, and daily counts of active, disabled and total user accounts. It never sees user names, usernames, passwords, questions, answers, files, audit logs or AI keys. See the Pharma AI privacy policy for details.
