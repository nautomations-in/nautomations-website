Pharma AI Privacy Policy

How N Automations handles data for Pharma AI version 1.1.0 for Windows. Your company's working data stays on your company's own server.

Effective date: 23-10-2026 (23 October 2026)

Applies to: Pharma AI version 1.1.0 for Windows (Microsoft Store and website versions), the N Automations Licence Service, and the e-mail verification step of setup.

1. Who we are

Pharma AI is made by N Automations, Hyderabad, India ("N Automations", "we", "us").

2. Summary

  • Your company's working data stays on your company's own server. This covers user accounts, questions, uploaded files, records of downloads, audit logs, settings, AI keys and, only if your Administrator switches answer storage on, AI answer text. N Automations has no account on that server and cannot read it.
  • We receive only licence data. Our Licence Service gets only what it needs to register your company, deliver the e-mail verification code, run the free trial, issue and check licences, and bill per active user account (section 4).
  • Your AI provider is your company's choice and contract. Questions go from your company server to the AI provider your company selected, using your company's own API key. They never pass through N Automations.
  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reports, advertising or tracking. We do not sell or rent any data.

3. Who is responsible for what

Your company decides how it uses Pharma AI and controls its company server and the data on it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, your company is the data fiduciary for that data: it gives notices to its employees, sets retention, handles its employees' requests and makes backups. N Automations does not process that content. N Automations decides how the licence data in section 4 is used and is responsible for it. This section is a summary, not legal advice.

5. Data N Automations never receives

  • Names of users, or usernames
  • Sign-in passwords and Level-2 passwords
  • Recovery codes and authenticator set-up keys
  • Questions (prompts) and AI answers
  • Uploaded files and their extracted text
  • Downloaded or created files
  • Audit logs and activity records
  • AI provider API keys
  • Model routes, token limits, budgets, branding, department set-up and other company settings
  • PC names, the PC list or any PC identifier (PCs never contact the Licence Service)
  • Any e-mail address other than the Administrator's company e-mail address
  • Telemetry, analytics, crash reports or advertising data. The app has none of these.

6. Data on your company server and PCs

  • All company data is stored only on your company's server. That server is either a Supabase project in your company's own account or a self-hosted server that your company runs.
  • AI answer text is not stored by default: only each answer's SHA-256 fingerprint, length, tokens and model are kept, unless your Administrator switches on answer storage for a set number of days.
  • Your Administrator controls retention: by default 1,095 days (3 years), within a range of 30 to 36,500 days. A nightly job at 02:00 IST applies it. Legal holds stop deletion. Deletions need a written reason and a password re-check, and are recorded in a permanent Deletion Log.
  • Backups of the company server are your company's responsibility.
  • On each PC, chat answers are kept only in memory and are gone at sign-out. The sign-in session, the PC connection details and the device key are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Files a user downloads are saved on that PC, where your company's own controls apply.
  • For questions about data on your company server, please contact your company's Administrator. N Automations cannot access that data.

7. AI providers

  • Your company chooses and contracts its AI provider: OpenAI, Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini). It uses its own API key.
  • AI providers are called only from your company server's AI Gateway. They are never called from PCs and never through N Automations.
  • For every allowed question, the AI provider receives the department instructions (which contain the company name, the app name and the department name), the question, recent turns of the same conversation, text extracted from attached files and, for pictures sent to a vision-capable model, a reduced image.
  • For the department check, a smaller "classifier" model chosen by your Administrator receives the question and, for uploads, the upload reason and description. This happens for every question that the first, rule-based stage does not refuse, including questions that are then refused. A question refused by the rule-based stage is not sent to any AI model.
  • The provider's handling of this data is governed by your company's own agreement with that provider. N Automations is not a party to that agreement.
  • We recommend paid business API accounts in your company's name, zero-data-retention or enterprise options where offered, regional options where required (Azure OpenAI allows an India region), and spending limits set in the provider's own console.

8. Service providers we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the N Automations Licence Service and its database, which holds the licence records in section 4. Worker logs, traces and metrics are switched off. As the hosting provider, Cloudflare carries the network traffic of these requests. At registration, we also use Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS for one mail-server (MX) lookup of your company e-mail domain, to refuse domains without mail.
  • Resend sends the 6-digit verification e-mail from info@nautomations.in. To do so, it receives the Administrator's company e-mail address and the code.
  • Supabase is the default company server platform. It runs in your company's own account under your company's own contract with Supabase. For automatic setup, and later company-server updates, we use our "Pharma AI" OAuth app on Supabase. It is used only from the Administrator's PC, and the Administrator can remove it in Supabase afterwards.
  • Microsoft provides Microsoft Store distribution and signing of the app package, installation through winget and Intune, and the Edge WebView2 runtime. The standard website installer downloads WebView2 from Microsoft only if it is missing. Microsoft's own privacy statement applies to your use of the Microsoft Store.
  • Cloud hosts for self-hosted company servers, such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, Oracle Cloud or Hetzner, are chosen and contracted by your company.

We use no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, advertising or CDN providers.

Cloudflare and Resend operate internationally, so the data in section 4 may be stored or processed outside India.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • We do not show advertising, use advertising IDs or embed trackers.
  • We do not collect telemetry, analytics or crash reports.
  • We do not access your company server. N Automations holds no credentials for it and has no back door.

10. Security

  • All traffic uses TLS.
  • Verification codes and network addresses are kept only as keyed hashes.
  • Licence data is signed with Ed25519, and daily reports are signed with HMAC.
  • Licence Service logging is switched off.
  • On PCs, the session, connection details and device key are protected with Windows DPAPI.
  • At every start, the app checks its own integrity (for the Store version, the Microsoft signature).

11. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask for a copy of the licence data we hold about your company, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to info@nautomations.in. Please write from, or name, the Administrator's company e-mail address so that we can verify the request. Deleting the company record ends the licence. Records that we must keep while a licence is active, or for legal and accounting reasons, are kept only for that purpose.
  • For a complaint about how we handle personal data, write to info@nautomations.in with the subject "Privacy".
  • Your Administrator can remove our Supabase OAuth app from your company's Supabase account at any time after setup.
  • Questions about data on your company server go to your company's Administrator.

12. Children

Pharma AI is business software for companies and their employees. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.
